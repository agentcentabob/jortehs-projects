Concept: LED-backed tactile ground surface indicators (TGSIs) that change colour as a vehicle approaches, so the platform edge itself signals what's happening. Driven by live Transport for NSW vehicle position data.

Key

What each colour on the platform edge means, in the order a passenger sees them. The muted control marks the state currently showing; press any control to hold that state, and press it again to return to live data.

Indicator State Meaning Controls
Steady yellow No vehicle at the platform. Standard tactile indicator colour.
Flashing red / orange Vehicle arriving. Begins as it approaches and continues about 10 seconds after it reaches the platform.
Flashing green / blue Doors opening. Runs for about 5 seconds.
Steady green Doors open, safe to board.
Flashing red / yellow Doors closing. Starts 30 seconds after the doors opened, or 30 seconds before the scheduled departure, whichever is later, then runs for 5 seconds.
Flashing red / orange Vehicle departing. Shares the arriving pattern. Runs from the moment the doors finish closing until it is seen at its next stop.
Steady blue The four lower rows. A fixed reference band that never changes, marking the edge itself.

Live vehicle positions

Vehicles currently reported on this platform's feed, plotted by GPS position.

Live data from the Transport for NSW Open Data Hub (Trip Planner departure_mon and GTFS-realtime Vehicle Positions). Map imagery © OpenStreetMap contributors. Line codes are shown only where the feed names one; most Sydney Trains services report an internal sector code instead, and appear as plain markers. Tactile indicator colours follow the concept sketch for this project; the dwell timings are modelled, as no public API reports vehicle door state.
