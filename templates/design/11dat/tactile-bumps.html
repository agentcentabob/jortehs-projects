Digital Tactile Bumps

Concept: LED-backed tactile ground surface indicators (TGSIs) that change colour as a train approaches, so the platform edge itself signals what's happening. Driven by live Transport for NSW vehicle position data.

Key

What each colour on the platform edge means, in the order a passenger sees them.

Indicator State Meaning
Steady yellow No train at the platform. Standard tactile indicator colour.
Flashing red / orange Train arriving. Begins as it approaches and continues about 10 seconds after it reaches the platform.
Flashing green / blue Doors opening. Runs for about 5 seconds.
Steady green Doors open, safe to board.
Flashing red / orange Doors closing. Starts 30 seconds after the doors opened, or 30 seconds before the scheduled departure — whichever is later.
Steady blue The four lower rows. A fixed reference band that never changes, marking the edge itself.

Live data from the Transport for NSW Open Data Hub (Trip Planner departure_mon and GTFS-realtime Vehicle Positions). Tactile indicator colours follow the concept sketch for this project; the dwell timings are modelled, as no public API reports train door state.
