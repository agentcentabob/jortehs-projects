Key

The simple logic behind each colour and pattern shown on the tactile bumps. The muted control marks the state currently showing: select any control to hold that state, and select it again to return to live data.

  • Steady yellow

    No vehicle at the platform. Standard tactile indicator colour.

  • Flashing red / orange

    Vehicle arriving. Begins as it approaches and continues about 10 seconds after it reaches the platform.

  • Flashing green / blue

    Doors opening. Runs for about 5 seconds.

  • Steady green

    Doors open, safe to board.

  • Flashing red / yellow

    Doors closing. Starts 30 seconds after the doors opened, or 30 seconds before the scheduled departure, whichever is later, then runs for 5 seconds.

  • Steady red

    Vehicle departing. Runs from the moment the doors finish closing until it has pulled clear of the platform.

  • Steady blue

    The four lower rows. A fixed reference band that never changes, marking the edge itself.
