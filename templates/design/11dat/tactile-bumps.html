Key

Logic behind each LED state and corresponding vehicle status. Select any state to demonstrate it on the simulation, and select it again to return to live data.

  • Steady yellow

    No vehicle at the platform. Default colour.

  • Flashing red / orange

    Vehicle arriving. Begins as it approaches and continues briefly upon reaching the platform.

  • Flashing green / blue

    Doors opening. Runs for about 10 seconds.

  • Steady green

    Doors open.

  • Flashing red / yellow

    Doors closing. Starts shortly before the scheduled departure, or after a given time depending on mode, whichever is later. Runs for 5 seconds.

  • Steady red

    Vehicle departing. Runs from the doors closing until it is clear of the platform.

  • Steady blue

    The four lower rows. A fixed reference band that never changes, and would not use LEDs.
